A kitchen with old roots

Afghan Imperial brings the cooking of Kabul, Herat and Mazar-i-Sharif to Alum Rock Road — rice perfumed with cardamom and orange peel, bread pulled straight from the tandoor, and stews built slowly over the course of a day, the way our grandmothers built them.

Afghanistan sat at the crossing point of the old Silk Road, and the kitchen still carries that history: saffron and dried fruit from Persia, dumplings that share a family with Central Asia's manti, warmth from the spice routes of the subcontinent. Everything here is cooked to order, unhurried.
